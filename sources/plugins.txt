Extrait de la page gwd, confirmant exactement les options déjà documentées dans plugins.txt/gwd.txt :

-plugin <PLUGIN>.cmxs
    load a safe plugin. Combined with -force to enable for every base. Combined with -unsafe
    to allow unverified plugins. e.g. "-plugin -unsafe -force".
-plugins <DIR>
    load all plugins in <DIR>. Combined with -force to enable for every base. Combined with
    -unsafe to allow unverified plugins. e.g. "-plugins -unsafe -force".
